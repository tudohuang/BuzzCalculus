使用手冊

最後更新：2026-09-08

這一頁把全部功能講完。趕時間的話：讀「30 秒版」和「作答」兩節就能上手， 其他等用到再回來查。這頁可以直接用瀏覽器列印存成 PDF。

30 秒版

BuzzCalculus 練的是看到題目三秒內知道用哪個工具的反射。 近 2,000 題依技巧分類、難度 R1–R6，答案全部經過獨立數值驗算。 沒有帳號、沒有伺服器：所有紀錄只存在你這台裝置的瀏覽器裡， 離線也能練。每天打開 → 按「開始訓練」→ 練完看回顧，就這樣。

第一次打開

會先問你的起點。建議做 8 題定位測驗（約 5 分鐘）： 做完會依表現設定難度、解鎖主線起點。不想做也可以自選階段， 起始難度會保守一點，實際練幾局之後系統會自動校準。 之後隨時可以在「設定 → 練習 → 定位」重測。

問完之後會有一段導覽，指著畫面上的按鈕說明每一區在做什麼， 五步走完就結束，隨時可以按「略過」。第一次作答時還會再指一次提示與計算紙。 想再看一次：設定 → 說明 → 再看一次導覽。

首頁「今天」

  • 今日任務：照你的每日目標（設定裡可調 5／10／12／20 題）排好的一份訓練，按下去就開始。抽題會自動偏向你的弱技巧、到期該回溫的技巧，並把不同題型交錯排開。
  • 每日一題：全站每天同一題，錯過就過了。
  • 宿敵小積：一個用你自己的速度紀錄養出來的虛擬對手，練得越快它越快。
  • 連勝與盾牌：連續有練就累積。覺得吵，設定裡開「專注模式」全部收起來 —— 連勝照算，只是不顯示。

訓練頁

功能是跟著練習慢慢出現的：剛開始只有「練習」一種， 練完第一局會多出弱點、模擬考與挑戰，三局之後首頁才出現每日一題、證明訓練、對戰與出題。 每次有東西解鎖都會講一句。不想等就到設定 → 介面 → 顯示全部功能一次打開。

常用的就三個模式：

  • 快速訓練：混合抽題，最短路徑進入練習。
  • 單題型訓練：只練極限／微分／積分／級數其中一類。
  • 練習模式：不計時的安靜練法。

高壓玩法收在下面的抽屜裡：大考模擬、壓力訓練、Boss 連戰、階梯測驗、 Integral Bee、無提示、生存……等。系統覺得今天適合某個模式時，會在按鈕上標出來。

「技巧包」可以指定範圍（換元、分部、三角代換、King's、多變數……）， 「本局設定」能調難度上限與答題方式。全部設定都會記住，下次直接沿用。

作答

兩種答題方式，訓練頁隨時可切：

  • 選擇題：四選一，點了就送出。適合手機、通勤、快節奏。
  • 自己寫：打進答案欄，下面有即時的算式預覽。這才是接近考試的練法。

「自己寫」的數學寫法（送出前看預覽確認就不會冤枉）：

  • 分數：1/2、3/4，別急著換小數 —— 0.5 也收，但 1/3 寫分數才精確。
  • 圓周率：pi，例如 pi/4、2*pi/3。
  • 根號：sqrt(2)、sqrt(3)/2。
  • 自然對數：ln(2)；自然底數：e、e^2。
  • 無限大：inf；集合寫 {1, -2}；區間寫 (0, 1)。
  • 收斂／發散這類判斷題，直接照題目給的選項字打或點選。

手機和平板上會用內建的數學鍵盤（十個數字、分數、pi、sqrt 都在上面）， 不會彈出系統鍵盤蓋住畫面；想用系統鍵盤，鍵盤上有切換鈕。 桌面按 ? 可以看快捷鍵。

每題有依難度給的建議時限倒數。可以跳過 —— 跳過和超時都會記進錯題本（標成「已跳過」／「超時」，和答錯分開看）， 之後重練答對就會自動從錯題本畢業（錯過越多次，要求的連對次數越多）。

計算紙

「自己寫」模式附手寫計算紙，iPad 加 Apple Pencil 體驗最好： 感壓筆跡、手掌不誤觸、長按不會跳選取。

  • 工具列有筆／橡皮擦／復原／重做／清空／全螢幕；清空後按一次復原就整張回來。
  • 筆寬（細／標準／粗）與紙面（白紙／黑板）在工具列或「設定 → 介面」都能調。
  • 草稿會自動保存：練完在回顧裡能看到每題當時的計算過程，下次遇到同一題也會還原。
  • 計算紙打開時倒數會暫停（大考模式除外）—— 慢慢算，不是在跟碼表打架。

提示與解法

每題有三層提示，從「往哪個方向想」逐步收斂到「關鍵一步」，最後一層也不會直接把答案講掉。 答完看得到參考解法。覺得題目有問題，題目右上角的「回報」會幫你把題號和相關資訊整理好。

錯題本

  • 答錯的題自動進錯題本，依主題分類，隨時「錯題重練」。
  • 練會的技巧過一陣子會排進回溫 —— 對抗遺忘曲線，不是罰你。
  • 可以整本列印（瀏覽器列印 → 存成 PDF），紙上複習。
  • 清除單題可以撤銷；清空整本會先跟你確認。

數據頁

能力雷達、各技巧的趨勢、預測分數、本週 vs 上週的週報，全部從本機紀錄算出來， 沒有任何數字送出去。週報可以匯出成圖片，也可以整理成一封報告寄給自己或家教。

題庫

全部題目都能瀏覽、搜尋（中文可以）、依主題／難度／技巧篩選，單題可以直接開練。 「自訂題」能自己出題（含預覽），用 #pack= 連結分享給同學 —— 對方點開就能練你出的題，資料一樣只存在各自的裝置上。

考前衝刺

模擬考分兩組。依考試範圍：期中模擬考極限與微分、期末模擬考積分與級數、 全範圍總複習兩邊都考 —— 期中與期末的差別是範圍，不是難度，兩張卷的難度區間一樣。 依考卷風格：轉學考、免修考、Integral Bee、小考節奏。

在「設定 → 練習 → 考前衝刺」設一個考試日期之後：首頁出現倒數卡（含分數預測）， 每日訓練自動偏向考試範圍裡你的缺口，最後一週切成衝刺配方。 考完清除會封存到歷史，不會弄丟紀錄。

資料與隱私

  • 所有紀錄只存在本機瀏覽器。換裝置：設定 → 資料 → 匯出 JSON，到新裝置匯入。匯入是合併不是覆蓋，兩邊練過的都會留著。
  • 清除資料會先確認，並講清楚會刪掉什麼（包含手寫草稿與進行中的存檔）。
  • 使用分析只送「哪個功能被用了幾次」的計數，不送作答內容；可以在設定裡關掉，關掉連分析程式都不會載入。
  • 選填的「校準包」只有題號、對錯次數、中位秒數這類統計，沒有任何可以認出你的東西。

這些承諾都有自動化測試在盯著。

離線與安裝

  • iPhone／iPad：Safari 分享鈕 → 「加入主畫面」。之後全螢幕開啟，沒訊號照樣出題。
  • Android／桌面 Chrome、Edge：網址列的「安裝」提示，或瀏覽器選單裡的「安裝應用程式」。
  • 裝完之後整站離線可用；連上網時會自動更新到最新版。

常見問題

R1–R6 是什麼？ 難度分級：R1–R2 基礎、R3 標準、R4 進階、R5–R6 大考深水區與競賽級。定位測驗與平常表現會決定你看到的範圍。

跳過會怎樣？ 會進錯題本，但標成「已跳過」—— 系統不會把它當成你「選錯方法」，只是記得這題還沒拿下。

答題音效怎麼關？ 設定 → 介面 → 答題音效。

為什麼答案可以相信？ 每題都跑過一條與解答無關的獨立數值驗算，不通過就不進題庫。細節在關於。

發現 bug 或題目怪怪的？ 題目右上角「回報」。

Guide

Last updated: 2026-09-30

This page covers every feature. In a hurry? Read "The 30-second version" and "Answering" and you're set; come back for the rest when you need it. You can print this page to PDF from your browser.

The 30-second version

BuzzCalculus trains the reflex of knowing which tool to use within three seconds of seeing a problem. Problems are sorted by technique, ranked R1–R6, and every answer has been independently checked numerically. No accounts, no server: all your records stay in this device's browser, and it works offline. Open it each day → press "Start training" → review when you're done. That's it.

First visit

You'll be asked where you're starting from. We recommend the 8-problem placement test (about 5 minutes): it sets your difficulty and unlocks your starting point on the main path. You can also pick a stage yourself; the starting difficulty will be a bit conservative, and the system recalibrates after a few sessions. You can retake it any time under Settings → Practice → Placement. New to calculus? Choose the beginner course instead: short lessons from functions through integrals.

Then a tour points at the buttons on screen and explains each area; it's five steps and you can skip it any time. Your first problem points out hints and the scratch paper once more. To see it again: Settings → Help → Replay the tour.

Home: "Today"

  • Today's quests: a set sized to your daily goal (5 / 10 / 12 / 20 problems, adjustable in Settings); press it to start. It leans toward your weak techniques and ones due for review, and interleaves problem types.
  • Daily one: the same problem for everyone each day. Miss it and it's gone.
  • Your rival: a virtual opponent built from your own speed records. The faster you get, the faster it gets.
  • Streaks and shields: keep practicing and they build up. Too noisy? Turn on Focus mode in Settings to hide them: streaks still count, they just aren't shown.

Train

Features appear as you practice: at first there's only Practice; after your first session you get weak spots, mock exams and challenges; after three sessions the home page adds the daily one, proof training, duels and problem writing. Each unlock is announced. Don't want to wait? Settings → Interface → Show all features turns everything on at once.

Three modes cover most use:

  • Quick session: mixed problems, the shortest way into practice.
  • Single-topic session: only limits, derivatives, integrals or series.
  • Practice mode: quiet, untimed practice.

High-pressure modes live in the drawer below: exam simulation, pressure training, boss rush, ladder test, Integral Bee, no hints, survival, and more. When the system thinks a mode suits you today, it marks the button.

"Technique packs" narrow the range (substitution, integration by parts, trig substitution, King's rule, several variables…), and "Session settings" set the difficulty cap and how you answer. Everything is remembered for next time.

Answering

Two ways to answer; switch any time on the Train page:

  • Multiple choice: pick one of four and it's submitted. Good for phones, commutes and fast pace.
  • Write it yourself: type into the answer box with a live preview underneath. This is the closest to a real exam.

How to type math (check the preview before submitting and you won't be marked wrong unfairly):

  • Fractions: 1/2, 3/4. No need to convert to decimals: 0.5 is accepted, but 1/3 is only exact as a fraction.
  • Pi: pi, e.g. pi/4, 2*pi/3.
  • Square roots: sqrt(2), sqrt(3)/2.
  • Natural log: ln(2); the number e: e, e^2.
  • Infinity: inf; sets: {1, -2}; intervals: (0, 1).
  • For converges / diverges questions, type or tap one of the options the problem gives.

On phones and tablets a built-in math keyboard appears (digits, fractions, pi and sqrt are all there) so the system keyboard doesn't cover the screen; a button on it switches to the system keyboard. On desktop, press ? for keyboard shortcuts.

Each problem has a suggested time limit based on its difficulty. You can skip: skips and timeouts go into the mistake notebook (marked "skipped" / "timed out", separate from wrong answers), and once you get them right in later practice they graduate out automatically (the more times you missed one, the more correct answers in a row it takes).

Scratch paper

"Write it yourself" comes with handwriting scratch paper; it works best on an iPad with Apple Pencil: pressure-sensitive ink, palm rejection, and no text-selection pop-ups on long press.

  • The toolbar has pen, eraser, undo, redo, clear and full screen; after clearing, one undo brings the whole page back.
  • Pen width (thin / normal / thick) and paper (white / blackboard) are in the toolbar or Settings → Interface.
  • Scratch work saves automatically: the review after a session shows your work for each problem, and it's restored the next time you meet the same problem.
  • The timer pauses while the scratch paper is open (except in exam mode): take your time, you're not racing a stopwatch.

Hints and solutions

Each problem has three levels of hints, narrowing from "which direction to think" to "the key step"; even the last one doesn't give the answer away. After answering you can see the reference solution. If a problem looks wrong, "Report" at its top right collects the problem number and details for you.

Mistake notebook

  • Problems you miss go into the mistake notebook automatically, sorted by topic; "Retry mistakes" any time.
  • Techniques you've learned come back for review after a while, to fight the forgetting curve, not to punish you.
  • You can print the whole notebook (browser print → save as PDF) and review on paper.
  • Removing a single problem can be undone; clearing the whole notebook asks you first.

Stats

Skill radar, per-technique trends, a predicted score, and a weekly report comparing this week with last, all computed from your local records; no number is sent anywhere. The weekly report can be exported as an image or written up as a report to send to yourself or a tutor.

Problem bank

Browse every problem, search, and filter by topic, difficulty or technique; any problem can be practiced directly. "Custom" lets you write your own (with a preview) and share them with a #pack= link: whoever opens it can practice your problems, and their data also stays on their own device.

Exam sprint

Mock exams come in two groups. By exam scope: the midterm mock covers limits and derivatives, the final mock covers integrals and series, and the full review covers both. Midterm and final differ in scope, not difficulty: both papers use the same difficulty range. By exam style: transfer exam, placement exam, Integral Bee, and quiz pace.

Set an exam date under Settings → Practice → Exam sprint and the home page shows a countdown card (with a score prediction), daily sessions lean toward the gaps in your exam scope, and the last week switches to a final-sprint mix. Clearing the date after the exam archives it to history; nothing is lost.

Data and privacy

  • All records stay in this browser only. Switching devices: Settings → Data → Export JSON, then import on the new device. Importing merges rather than overwrites, so practice from both sides is kept.
  • Clearing data asks first and spells out what will be deleted (including handwritten scratch work and in-progress saves).
  • Analytics only sends counts of which features were used, never your answers; you can turn it off in Settings, and then the analytics script isn't even loaded.
  • The optional "calibration pack" contains only stats like problem numbers, right/wrong counts and median seconds, nothing that could identify you.

Automated tests keep watch over all of these promises.

Offline and install

  • iPhone / iPad: Safari's Share button → "Add to Home Screen". It then opens full screen and serves problems without a signal.
  • Android / desktop Chrome, Edge: the "Install" prompt in the address bar, or "Install app" in the browser menu.
  • Once installed, the whole site works offline; it updates to the latest version when you're online.

FAQ

What are R1–R6? Difficulty ranks: R1–R2 basic, R3 standard, R4 advanced, R5–R6 hard exam and competition level. The placement test and your everyday results decide the range you see.

What happens if I skip? It goes into the mistake notebook marked "skipped": the system doesn't treat it as picking the wrong method, it just remembers you haven't got this one yet.

How do I turn off answer sounds? Settings → Interface → Answer sounds.

Why can I trust the answers? Every problem goes through an independent numerical check that doesn't use the solution; problems that fail don't make it into the bank. Details on the About page.

Found a bug or a strange problem? "Report" at the top right of the problem.
