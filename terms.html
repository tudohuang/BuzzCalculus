服務條款

最後更新：2026-09-16

這是一個練微積分的工具：無帳號、紀錄存在你自己的裝置上。 我們盡力讓題目與答案正確，但它不是你的老師，也不保證你會考好。

使用範圍

個人學習、備考、課堂使用、把題目印進講義（請註明來源）都可以。你的紀錄可以隨時匯出，想怎麼處理都行。

請不要把整份題庫抓下來重新包裝販售、用程式大量請求影響其他人，或在自訂題裡放侵權與無關的內容再分享出去。

題目與答案

每一題都標了來源類型：原創、改編、取材自某種風格、公有領域。 標成「某某風格」的題目是我們自己寫的原創題，不是任何學校或考試機構的官方試題，我們也與它們無關。

答案有自動化驗算把關（目前約一半的題目能被獨立驗算），難度等級 R1–R6 是我們自己的評估。 即使如此，我們不保證每一題都完全正確；發現問題請用題目旁的回報按鈕告訴我們。

沒有保證與責任

服務以現況提供，不保證不中斷或無錯誤。你的練習紀錄只存在瀏覽器裡：清除瀏覽器資料、換裝置、瀏覽器自行回收空間，都會讓紀錄消失，我們無法救回，在意的話請定期匯出 JSON。 在法律允許的最大範圍內，我們不對使用或無法使用本服務造成的損失負責。

變更與聯絡

我們可能調整或停止本服務；沒有帳號系統，重大變更會更新這一頁的日期。有問題請用每一題右上角的「回報」。

Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-09-30

This is a tool for practicing calculus: no accounts, and your records stay on your own device. We work hard to keep problems and answers correct, but it isn't your teacher and doesn't promise you a good grade.

What you can do

Personal study, exam prep, classroom use, and putting problems in handouts (please credit the source) are all fine. You can export your records at any time and do what you like with them.

Please don't scrape the whole problem bank to repackage it, flood the site with automated requests, or share custom problems containing infringing or unrelated content.

Problems and answers

Every problem is labeled with its source type: original, adapted, written in a certain style, or public domain. Problems labeled "in the style of" an exam are our own originals, not official problems of any school or exam body, and we have no connection to them.

Answers are checked by automated verification, and the R1–R6 difficulty ranks are our own judgment. Even so, we don't guarantee every problem is correct; if you find a mistake, please tell us with the report button next to the problem.

No warranty or liability

The service is provided as is, without any promise that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Your records live only in your browser: clearing browser data, switching devices, or the browser reclaiming storage will erase them, and we can't recover them. If they matter to you, export the JSON regularly. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for losses from using or being unable to use the service.

Changes and contact

We may change or stop the service; with no accounts, significant changes update the date on this page. Questions: use "Report" at the top right of any problem.
